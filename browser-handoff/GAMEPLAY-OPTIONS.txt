GAMEPLAY DIRECTIONS FOR BAHAYA JEREBU

1. HOSE ROUTES + CARDS — selected by the owner
Draw a water route through a small board, play a firefighting card through it,
then watch the boss's announced response. Wet ground makes later routes cheaper.
The fun comes from setting up combinations, saving the village and outsmarting
the boss, rather than walking to each fire. Tap-by-tap routing works as well as
dragging. Best fit for deliberate mobile play, short runs and later co-op.
Risk: too many numbers or repeated route drawing. Keep three actions per turn,
short paths, instant previews and a replay-last-route shortcut.

2. DRAG-AND-DROP CREW DISPATCH
Drop a firefighter, pump and rescue team onto lanes. Teams act automatically;
pause to reassign them before telegraphed boss attacks. Night becomes defense,
camp becomes a compact build screen. More lively and spectator-friendly, but
harder to balance and less comfortable on a small phone without pause.

3. AIM-AND-RELEASE WATER CANNON
Pull back to aim an arc of water. Bounce shots off wind currents, fill tanks,
and hit boss vents. Each turn grants three shots. Very immediate and funny,
with strong boss reactions. Physics tuning and precise touch aim are the risk.

4. RHYTHM PUMP BATTLES
Tap a short pump rhythm to charge water, then choose a target/counter card.
Boss attacks introduce call-and-response patterns. Strong personality and audio
identity, but audio-off play and accessibility need a parallel non-rhythm input.

RECOMMENDED EXPERIENCE
Working subtitle: Bahaya Jerebu! — Hose & Havoc.
Tagline: Plan the hose. Soak the chaos. Save the kampung.

Example turn:
The boss announces a west-to-east ember gust. Draw a hose through two wet tiles
to reach a vent cheaply. Play Foam Line to protect the threatened corridor.
Use the last action to refill. The gust hits the foam instead of the village.
Next turn, douse the second vent and use Burst Jet on the exposed boss core.

Humor belongs in original monsters, expressions and short reactions:
- Bara Bebola: an overconfident ember ball whose bounced attack can be blocked.
- Gambut Gempal: a grumpy peat mound that needs repeated soaking, not one hit.
- Raja Jerebu: a floating smoke tyrant who poses dramatically, then deflates
  like a squeaky balloon when its core gets drenched.
These are proposed original names, not a trademark clearance claim.

Keep jokes brief and skippable. No scrolling meme text during planning, no
borrowed viral character designs, no unlicensed meme audio, and no jokes at the
expense of people experiencing haze or needing rescue.
