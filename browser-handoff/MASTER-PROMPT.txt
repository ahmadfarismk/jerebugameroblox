THE SIGNATURE MECHANIC: HOSE ROUTING
The player does not walk an avatar. They manage a crew from a compact 6x6 board.
Select a card, draw a hose from an active hydrant through adjacent tiles to its
target, preview the full result, then confirm. The hose visibly carries water
along the selected route and changes the board. Existing wet tiles reduce the
cost of future routes. Routes, card combos and boss intent are the game.
Support both drag routing and tap successive tiles. Right-click/Escape cancels;
provide a visible touch Cancel button. Never require dragging as the only input.
Nothing damages the player while they are thinking. Every enemy action is
telegraphed before the player commits End Turn.

RULES FOR THE FIRST IMPLEMENTATION
Use these as explicit starting balance, not as empirically proven values.
- Coordinates: row 0 is top, row 5 bottom, column 0 left. Cardinal adjacency.
- Board terrain: ground, tree, rock, hydrant, house, peat and boss nozzle.
  Fire, wet and foam are separate tile states, not conflicting terrain types.
- Starting hydrant: (5,2). Start with 10 water (capacity 10), 3 actions/turn,
  5 camp integrity, API 30, and zero Camp Fund. There is no moving avatar HP.
  Reuse the heart icon for CAMP INTEGRITY, explicitly labelled as such.
- One route begins at an active hydrant and has at most 8 edges. No diagonal
  steps, repeated tiles, rocks or houses. An occupied fire/vent tile may be the
  final target but not an intermediate tile. Source tile is free.
- Route cost = card's base water + 1 per entered dry tile; entered wet tiles
  cost 0. Display the exact total before confirming. Wet is boolean and does
  not stack. Invalid routes consume nothing. Confirmed routes cannot be undone.
- A successful water action wets its traversed ground and target for two enemy
  resolutions, unless a stated boss attack dries them. Wet prevents ignition.
- Any living fire has 2 HP initially; extinguishing awards 4 personal coins and
  +1 Camp Fund, preserving the original 25% bonus concept. Personal coins are
  the run score/cosmetic progression, not a second mandatory spending system.
- Refill is ALWAYS available: spend 1 action, restore 5 water up to capacity.
  It does not require drawing a card or a route. End Turn is always available.
- Ordinary campaign encounters win when all fires are out and required rescues
  are completed. Boss encounters win when boss HP reaches zero.
- Lose at camp integrity 0 or API 301+ for two consecutive enemy resolutions.
  Show the first hazardous strike as an explicit evacuation warning.
- API is a game meter, not a medical simulation. Use the existing project's
  display bands: 0–50 Good, 51–100 Moderate, 101–200 Unhealthy, 201–300 Very
  Unhealthy, 301+ Hazardous. Preserve their blue/green/yellow/orange/red colors.
  Each fire left alive adds 8 API per enemy resolution, each extinction removes
  6 API. Clamp 0–500. A fully fire-free resolution removes another 10 API.
- A house or camp target hit by an unblocked telegraphed attack loses 1 integrity.
  Do not apply invisible adjacency damage as well. The preview must show damage.
- No random spread surprises: generate the next enemy intent at the END of an
  enemy resolution and display it throughout the next player planning phase.

TURN RESOLUTION ORDER
1. Start player turn: 3 actions, draw to 5 cards, show upcoming enemy intent.
2. Player may route, confirm cards, refill, or end early. Previews do not mutate
   state. Committing an action atomically validates then spends resources.
3. End Turn locks input. Resolve the already-displayed enemy attack; foam/wet
   counters apply. Compute fire/API/integrity changes and check loss.
4. Decrement wet/foam duration AFTER it has protected against this resolution.
   Remove expired effects. Resolve victory/encounter transition.
5. If still playing, discard remaining hand and generate/display the NEXT enemy
   intent. Return to step 1 to replenish actions and draw exactly once.
   Newly added telegraphs never hit during the same resolution that creates them.
Use a seeded PRNG and a deterministic pure rules engine so previews match play.

STARTER DECK AND CARDS
Deck: Spray x3, Burst Jet x2, Foam Line x2, Soak x2, Wind Turn x1, Rescue x1.
Draw pile and discard are real. Played cards go to discard. When draw pile is
empty, shuffle discard with the seeded PRNG; do not duplicate cards. Tutorial
hands may be authored; label that as tutorial logic. Post-tutorial draws should
be deterministic for the seed but varied between runs.

Spray: 1 action, base water 0, route to target, deal 2 fire damage; cannot hit core.
Burst Jet: 2 actions, base water 1, route, deal 4 fire/vent/core damage. Core only
when exposed. One target, no hidden splash damage.
Foam Line: 1 action, base water 1, route to empty tile; foam all route tiles for
two resolutions. Blocks ignition and one ember impact per foamed tile, consuming
that tile's foam on impact. It does not damage the boss directly.
Soak: 1 action, base water 1, route to peat; suppresses peat and places a soaked
marker. A second Soak on the immediately following player turn extinguishes it.
Miss that turn and it reignites at 2 HP. Ordinary Spray can suppress peat for
one turn but cannot award peat rewards or permanently clear it.
Wind Turn: 1 action, no water, no route; rotate the announced directional ember
attack 90 degrees clockwise. Preview affected tiles. A directional intent may
be rotated once per turn, never repeatedly to erase the threat.
Rescue: 1 action, no water; select one threatened house with a cardinal path of
non-burning, non-rock tiles to an active hydrant (houses are allowed endpoints).
Move its residents to safety in a short animation, complete that rescue, award
+2 Camp Fund. This is a finite objective, never repeatable farming.

Keep an always-available 1-action Emergency Spray with route cost +1 base water
and 1 damage as a fallback when the deck contains no useful water card. The
normal Spray is more efficient. Prevent unwinnable hands without auto-winning.

CAMP UPGRADES — WEB BALANCE, NOT ROBLOX PRICES
Camp purchases are one-time per run, paid from the single Camp Fund; no timers.
Water Tower, cost 3: +4 max water and +2 water per Refill, applied immediately.
Field Kitchen, cost 3: restore 2 integrity now and 1 on each later camp visit,
up to max 5. A repeated render/reload must not repeat the camp-visit heal.
Rescue Shelter, cost 4: once per run prevent integrity loss below 1, then consume
its rescue charge; show clearly whether the charge is ready or spent.
Offer Skip. Grey out unaffordable upgrades with readable reason. Never require
all upgrades to win. In the tutorial slice award a completion stipend so one
upgrade is affordable; record it visibly, not as an unexplained balance change.

RAJA JEREBU — A REAL BOSS WITH COUNTERPLAY
An original goofy dark-purple cloud tyrant with expressive eyes and a lime core.
The illustrated sprite is presentation, not the authoritative hitbox. Position
it above the board with a labelled health/shield display, not over route tiles.
Boss HP 12. Vents at (1,1) and (1,4), 2 HP each. Core nozzle at (0,2). Hydrant at
(5,2); keep at least one legal route to each objective in every authored layout.
Each turn announces the exact attack tiles and effect before player input.

Closed core: while either vent is active, core damage is zero; invalid core shots
must be rejected before spending. Extinguishing both vents opens the core NOW.
Burst Jet routed to the nozzle then deals 4 core damage. The core stays open
through the NEXT player turn, giving a guaranteed opportunity to draw/refill.
At the end of that next player turn, relight both vents and close the core.
Represent the window with an explicit turn counter, not animation duration.
For the introductory boss hand sequence, ensure at least one Burst Jet is
available during each opening; later encounters can use the normal deck.

Cycle attacks: Ember Row (announced row, foam/wet counters), Dry Gust (announced
column loses wet at resolution, no immediate fire damage), and Peat Pop (two
announced eligible ground tiles ignite at resolution unless wet/foamed).
One attack per enemy resolution. Below 6 HP, attack patterns cover one extra
tile but remain fully announced; do not invalidate a turn already being planned.
The boss cannot change a selected intent mid-turn because its HP crossed a
threshold. No unavoidable full-board damage or pure HP sponge design.

Give it a short goofy wind-up, a squash/stretch hit reaction, an angry puff at
half HP and a comic deflation defeat. Use animation transforms/particles around
the supplied static sprite; do not claim that one PNG is an animated sprite sheet.
Original short optional lines: 'WHO TURNED ON THE TAP?!' and 'My majestic haze!'
Subtitles and mute controls; no copyrighted meme sound clips or forced voice.
