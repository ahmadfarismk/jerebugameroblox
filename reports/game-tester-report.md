# Game Tester report: round 5 (multiplayer features), 2026-10-18

## 1. Verdict
**No-ship yet. One fix needed, and real multiplayer is still untested.** The solo side of round 5 works in play: the new HUD layout, the SHOP button, the station counter and camp kiosk, the ping wheel (Q tap and hold, touch), downed solo, haze drain per band and mask, and the core loop. Fix M1 before shipping: a downed player can still mount a ride, and the Truck keeps putting out fires. Also fix or accept M2 (saves) and M3 (the Roblox player list overlapping the right-hand column).
**Two-client testing could not be done.** The MCP can only start a solo playtest. I tried to start Test → Clients and Servers through computer use, and the access request was denied. So the revive prompt, the crew panel with real players, the scout reward and the coin split were checked by reading the code only.
**Important: Studio DataStore access is ON now.** It is not off as the brief says. My test session loaded and saved the account's real `JerebuSave_v2` record (see M2). I put the record back the way it was (coins 36, nothing bought, no lock).

## 4. What passed
- **HUD desktop (1530x520):** the day pill and API card sit in the top strip, with Health above Water under the API card. The left column (coins, menu, SHOP) is below the 58 px inset. Toasts and the seeding banner hang under the day pill. Nothing in our own UI overlaps (QA5_HUD_Desktop).
- **HUD 375x647 portrait (device sim, touch on):** coins/menu, day pill and SHOP on the left; API, Health and Water on the right. The mask hint wraps. Toasts sit under the columns. The PING button is above the jump button and clear of the joystick. No clipping (QA5_Portrait, QA5_PortraitBusy).
- **Shop from all three entry points with real input:** the SHOP button (bought Boots Lv2), the station counter E prompt (bought Pressure Lv2) and the camp kiosk E prompt (bought Tank Lv2, MaxWater 150). E works when the camera faces the prompt. Earlier "E fails" reports were the camera facing away.
- **Pings with real input:** Q tap sends a Fire ping at the cursor, and a second tap 0.38 s later is dropped (1.5 s cooldown). Q hold opens the wheel at the cursor (QA5_PingWheel). Moving right and releasing sends Water. The touch PING button opens the wheel and tapping Help sends it. The `PingPillar_*` pillar is visible in capture (orange column, QA5_PingFire) and is replaced per player. The `PingMarker` BillboardGui exists (AlwaysOnTop) but **is not drawn in `screen_capture`**, same as the fire markers, so the markers are not checked by eye.
- **Ping exploits refused:** bad kind, nil, number, lowercase or "\0" kinds; nil, string or table positions; NaN; ±inf; 600 studs away; 51 pings in one frame (only 1 accepted).
- **Downed:** spraying is blocked (Spraying=false, water unchanged) and the hose is unequipped on collapse. The banner counts down "Heading back to the BOMBA station · 7s" (QA5_DownedBanner).
- **Core loop:** a fire sprayed out in 2.1 s gave +12 coins and Padam +1. Hydrant refill works. Peat smoulders after 3.0 s, soaks out 2.6 s later and gives +20. Cloud seeding (real E hold): rain starts and API falls. DebugSkip ran the days through to the Emergency ending (8/21 stars, chart, QA5_Ending). Masks, Bicycle and Truck passes were bought through the Shop remote. Server log has no errors except plugin and UIBlox noise. No "player tick failed".

## Lead follow-up (round 5 fixes)
Fixed and verified in a solo Studio playtest (Studio and src match byte for byte):
- **M1:** riding refused while downed ("You can't ride while collapsed"); truck cannon skips downed players.
- **M2:** Studio uses its own store `JerebuSave_v2_Studio` (and skips the v1 legacy store). Verified the real `u96592687` record stayed at 36 coins / no lock while this playtest granted 2000 debug coins.
- **M3:** new `StarterPlayerScripts.ClientRules` hides Roblox's PlayerList (verified `GetCoreGuiEnabled(PlayerList) == false`) and disables your own RevivePrompt locally.
- **M4–M6 (code):** load aborts if the player left mid-retry and releases any lock it took; a failed v1 read releases the lock; release-on-leave retries 3x; `write()` returns true only when our data was actually written (so ProcessReceipt can't grant on a lost lock). Remaining: coins earned during a slow load are still dropped silently.
- **M7:** a downed player who heals to 40 HP (e.g. inside the station) recovers instead of dying — verified collapse → station → Downed false at 49 HP, same character.
- **M8:** scout bonus needs the pinger within 200 studs of the fire and caps at 10 per player per day; pings more than 150 studs above/below you are rejected.
- Collapse toast now goes to teammates only, with device-neutral text ("Go revive them.").
Still open: SHOP button can't close the shop (backdrop covers it), downed banner over the collapse toast, real 2-player revive / scout / coin split (needs a multi-client run — Codex), live session-lock test.
